## Project Description

This project focuses on the development of a web-based knowledge system for cardiovascular disease prevention. The system combines a React frontend, an Express/Prisma backend, and a PostgreSQL database to provide standardized, bilingual (EN/RU) disease cards with consistent descriptions, risk factors, symptoms, and prevention guidance.

The project emphasizes production-ready architecture with containerization, comprehensive testing, observability, and API-first design suitable for future expansion.

The research component includes analysis of real-world cardiovascular datasets (UCI Heart Disease, Statlog, Cleveland, Hungary) containing clinical data from actual patients. This data-driven approach ensures that the prevention recommendations and risk factor information are grounded in validated medical research rather than theoretical assumptions.

The project carries significant social value by making evidence-based cardiovascular health information accessible to the general public, potentially contributing to early disease awareness and prevention in communities where such knowledge may be limited.
